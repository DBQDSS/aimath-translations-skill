# Local MathTranslations Guide

Use these local resources when choosing MathTranslations formatting or a
terminology starting point. They require no website access. A supplied template
and the source edition take precedence over these maintained notes.

## Production Principles

Preserve the source's meaning, mathematical structure, numbering, and dependencies.
Resolve ambiguity from the original PDF; extraction is a working draft. Prefer
precise hypotheses and logical relationships over fluent paraphrase. Use one
context-appropriate term consistently and retain its English form at introduction
when helpful. Create clickable references instead of typing printed numbers.

For MathTranslations presentation, record title, author, edition, publisher,
translator, model, and revision date. Use Song for prose/examples, Kai for new
terms, Fang for theorem-like text, and a serif Latin font. Follow the inspected
template's punctuation and terminology-index conventions. Extract, translate,
compile, and compare with the source in reviewable units; check mathematics,
terminology, numbering, and layout before delivery.
