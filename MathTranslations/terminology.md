# Local Terminology Reference

[terminology.tsv](terminology.tsv) is a small, editorial starter glossary for
mathematics and AI/ML translations. Its columns are `source_term`, `chinese`,
`sense`, and `note`. It is maintained in this repository, not an export of the
MathTranslations community database or an assertion of community consensus.

Use it without fetching a website:

1. Identify the source definition and field before looking up a spelling.
2. Match the sense column; one spelling can have multiple senses and translations.
3. Apply a supplied/project glossary when its choice is deliberate and documented.
4. For uncovered or disputed terms, use the source definition and locally supplied
   references, retain the English term at first introduction, and record the choice
   and uncertainty. Lack of website access must not stop translation or invite a
   fabricated consensus. Use online research only when explicitly requested.
5. Preserve model/dataset/API identifiers, literal prompts, and code tokens. Do not
   run a global TSV substitution over prose, mathematics, or listings.

The official terminology download endpoints redirected to a login page when
checked on 2026-10-08. No complete database has been imported. The starter glossary
can be extended in the project without changing the bundled reference. Any future
community export is an explicit maintenance task: record its provenance, date,
license, sense/status fields, and compatibility before distributing it.

Source URLs and the snapshot scope are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json).
